About Sean

I'm a creative copywriter and digital marketer who never really left the band room. I met my wife Jillian in middle school jazz band, and these days she's my partner in life and in business. We run our shop out of Doylestown, Pennsylvania.

I'm a lifelong musician and a proud nerd. You'll find me at the Renaissance faire, hunched over a Nintendo 64 controller, or dragging friends to some spooky historical spot I read about at 2am. I've even taken the mic for a few rounds of standup, which turns out to be excellent training for writing copy that lands.

All of it feeds the work. Rhythm, timing, a good hook and knowing exactly when to land the punchline are the same skills whether you're on a stage or on a sales page.

Picking Drexel's Music Industry program over Berklee was one of the best decisions I ever made. It's a fiercely selective program that treats music as both an art and a serious business, and it packed in far more than most people expect from a music degree.

I studied everything from contract law to how royalties actually get split and paid out to artists. I learned the machinery behind the music, not just how to make it. On top of the business side, I trained to produce sound for film at a professional level, which is exactly what grew into my Amazing Audio Ads business.

That mix, real business fundamentals plus hands-on creative and technical craft, is the foundation everything else here is built on.
