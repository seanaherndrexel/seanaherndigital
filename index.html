About

Who I am.

I am a copywriter and marketer who runs Main Street Creative in Doylestown with my wife and business partner, Jillian.

For over ten years I have made advertising and marketing for Bucks County businesses and clients all over the world. The work is simple to describe and hard to do well: copy that says the specific thing a customer needs to hear, design that is unmistakably yours, and marketing that reaches the right people instead of everyone and no one.

I have written landing pages, email funnels, sales letters, advertorials, and ad scripts across just about every industry you can name. I also produce audio, which grew into my radio and audio ad studio, Amazing Audio Ads.

Jillian and I met as kids in middle school jazz band, and music never left me. I am an avid musician, a lifelong nerd who loves the Renaissance faire, retro Nintendo 64 games, and hunting down spooky historical places. I have even done some standup. These days home is Doylestown, with Jillian and our three cats, Alphonse, Winny, and Maka.

Master of Business Administration & Music Industry

DREXEL UNIVERSITY · PHILADELPHIA, PA

I chose Drexel's Music Industry program over Berklee College of Music, and it shaped the way I work today. The program is one of the most respected of its kind, and it went far beyond making music.

I studied contract law, learned exactly how royalties are distributed to artists, and trained to produce sound for film at a professional level. That production training is what led directly to my audio advertising business, Amazing Audio Ads. Paired with my MBA, it is the reason I can sit on both sides of a project: the creative and the business.
